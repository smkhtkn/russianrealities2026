The Country and The World: Russian Realities 2026

Berlin / November 20–21

On November 20–21, 2026, the Ninth International Conference «Country and World: Russian Realities-2026» will take place. From 2018 to 2021, the conferences were held by the Sakharov Center in Moscow. Since 2022, the conference has been held in Berlin.

Political scientists, sociologists, and economists will discuss the transformation of the Russian political regime, its ideology and repressive policies, the wartime economy, shifts in public sentiment, and the transformation of international relations.

Recordings of all conference presentations and discussions will be published on the «Country and World» YouTube channel.
