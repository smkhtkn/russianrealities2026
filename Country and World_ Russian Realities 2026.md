**Country and World: Russian Realities 2026**

***Ninth Annual Conference***

Organized by the "[Country and World](https://sakharov.world/)" Project (Sakharov Review)

November 20–21, 2026

Berlin, Germany 

From 2018 to 2021, the conferences were hosted by the Sakharov Center in Moscow. Political scientists, sociologists, and economists will discuss the transformation of the Russian political regime, its ideology and repressive policies, the wartime economy, shifts in public sentiment, and the transformation of international relations.

Recordings of all conference presentations and discussions will be published on the [”Country and World](https://www.youtube.com/@Ostraneimire)” YouTube channel.
